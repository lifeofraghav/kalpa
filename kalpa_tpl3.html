08 / Questions

Asked, answered.

Why not just eat pomegranates?

Only one in three people host the gut bacteria that convert pomegranate into Urolithin A, and even converters can't eat their way to the 500 mg trial dose. Myvo delivers the finished molecule, so your microbiome stops being the bottleneck.

Will I feel anything?

Probably not. Urolithin A is not a stimulant; its effects showed up in trials as measured strength and endurance over months. That is why membership includes the day-0 to day-120 protocol: decide on your data, not on vibes.

Yes. Myvo holds the Indian license to import and sell, and sources Mitopure® directly from its maker. Every carton carries the importer details and batch assay Indian law requires, which grey-market bottles cannot show you.
