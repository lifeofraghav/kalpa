Inside one of your muscle cells

  • Worn mitochondria

This is where your energy comes from.

Every muscle cell carries hundreds of mitochondria. They turn food and oxygen into the fuel behind every rep, every stride, every flight of stairs.

From your thirties, some of them wear out.

Worn mitochondria make less energy and leak more waste, and the cell gets slower at clearing them out. You feel it as heavier legs and longer recoveries.

Urolithin A flags them for recycling.

It switches mitophagy back on, the cell's own clean-up: each worn mitochondrion is sealed in a membrane, broken down, and its parts reused.

New ones take their place.

Fresh, efficient mitochondria grow in the space the worn ones left. This is the renewal the trials measured.
