The First Ninety: CMO operating plan.

Build the want before the warehouse. Ninety days to a thousand-person waitlist, a hundred founding members ready to convert, and a proof engine no competitor can copy.

Owner Raghav Dua, CMOCadence 3 sprints x 30 daysAug to Nov 2026
Operating principles

Four rules that survive all ninety days.

Sell nothing until the license is clean. We build demand, community and proof. Inventory waits for paper. The waitlist is the product until batch 001.
The founder is the funnel. My face, my protocol, my numbers, daily. Organic content is our CAC advantage; paid ads are a Sprint 3 experiment, not a crutch.
Proof over promises. Every claim traces to a published trial. Every batch will trace to a signed lab report. We market the standard, not the miracle.
Community before customers. A hundred people who know us beat ten thousand who scrolled past. WhatsApp group first, checkout later.

Sprint 1: Foundation and signal

Days 1 to 30

Close the paper (with Mohit)

  • Timeline agreement reviewed, license number verified on FoSCoS, entity and equity term sheet signed. Day 14 hard gate.
  • Trademark filings (Kalpa wordmark + कल्प), FoSCoS and LMPC applications started. Cheap, immediate.

Stand up the machine

  • @kalpa handles, domain, link-in-bio, scheduling stack, waitlist form wired to WhatsApp community. Site is live; add the waitlist capture. Day 10.
  • Hire: one video editor on retainer, one community/ops VA by day 30.
  • The Baseline Shoot: my full day-0 protocol on camera: grip, chair-rise, timed carry, VO2, blood panel at the lab. This is the launch asset and the template every member follows.

Content engine: three pillars

  • Deep Time Diaries: my n=1 protocol, weekly numbers, no filter.
  • The Honest Molecule: science explainers: why 2 in 3 people can't make Urolithin A, what a real trial looks like, how to read a COA.
  • The Counterfeit Files: teardown genre on fake supplements and paper-thin certificates, no names, all standards.
  • Cadence: 4 reels/week on @lifeofraghav, 3 repurposed on @kalpa, one long piece weekly.

Seed the hundred

  • Personal DMs to the 50 warmest followers and Hyrox contacts into a private founding WhatsApp group. Zero broadcast, all personal.
Exit gate

300 waitlist / 100 in community / paper signed / baseline film shot. Miss the paper gate and Sprint 2 continues on content only; nothing brand-committed ships.

Sprint 2: Audience to community

Days 31 to 60

Own the Hyrox room

  • Film at every reachable event; athlete interview series with the longevity hook. Partner with 2 to 3 boxes/coaches for shoot access.
  • Ambassador ten: shortlist 10 athletes; offer founding membership and future rev share, no paid-post deals. Their baselines become content.

Turn attention into a list

  • Weekly Longevity Lab live (IG + WhatsApp): Q&A, protocol reviews. Bring Mohit on once: the builder who takes what he sells.
  • 5-part WhatsApp/email nurture: mitophagy 101, the converter lottery, the trials, the proof standard, the founding offer.
  • PR without an agency: pitch 3 podcasts (fitness + founder), pitch one journalist the counterfeit story: $5.3M US judgment, India parallel, our standard as the fix.

Product readiness

  • Packshoot with real packaging samples; unboxing-ready founding member kit designed (numbered vessel, COA card, protocol booklet).
  • NABL lab selected and assay spec locked (95 to 105%, HPLC-UV); Informed Sport application scoped and budgeted.
Exit gate

1,000 waitlist / 500 nurture list / 2 podcasts recorded / 10 ambassadors confirmed. Day 60: license status review with Mohit decides Sprint 3 mode.

Sprint 3: Convert

Days 61 to 90

Mode A: ingredient approval clean → ship

  • Founding Hundred drop: 72-hour window, numbered vessels 001 to 100, founding price locked for life.
  • Batch 001 COA published before a single box ships. Informed Sport application announced publicly.
  • I onboard every member personally: day-0 protocol call, measurement kit, WhatsApp cohort. Retention is designed here, not later.
  • Paid experiment: max ₹50k retargeting warm audiences only. Organic stays the engine.

Mode B: approval pending → deepen the moat

  • Batch 001 reservation list: no deposits, founding price locked for first 100 names. Scarcity without selling.
  • The Baseline Cohort: run the measurement protocol with the 10 ambassadors, product-free. We baseline India's athletes before we ship anything. Nobody can copy a moat made of other people's data and trust.
  • Publish The Kalpa Standard: our public page defining what a real COA is. It becomes the category's reference and our permanent SEO asset.

Either mode

  • Referral mechanic: founding members gift 2 invites. Day-90 review: retention dashboard, CAC/LTV first read, M4 to 12 budget proposal to Mohit.
Exit gate

Mode A: 100 members, 60%+ complete day-0 protocol, CAC near zero. Mode B: 250 reservations, 25 athletes baselined. Either way: the brand enters month 4 with proof, list and momentum.
